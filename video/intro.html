📝 English
- please explain me shortly what is a race condition
+ Please briefly explain to me what a race condition is.
• explain to me: "explain" needs "to" before the person.
• what a race condition is: statement word order inside a question.
• briefly, not "shortly", which means "soon".
⬆️ C1: "Could you give me a concise explanation of race conditions?"
Think in Vietnamese. Learn the English.

+ Briefly explain the difference between let and const in JavaScript.
• briefly: fits "ngắn gọn" better than "shortly", which means "soon".
• the difference between X and Y: the usual way to compare two things.
